Given a toilet paper roll, a straw, a balloon, paper clips and tape, we had to get a Lego figure across a string. Our design taped the balloon, the tube, the figure and a cut length of straw together — the balloon for thrust, the straw riding the line. It carried 28 feet, tied for second.
