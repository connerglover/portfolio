In this challenge we were given a toilet paper roll, a straw, a balloon, paper clips, and tape. We had to build something to get a lego man across a string. Our final design was a balloon, toilet paper roll, the Lego man, and a cut straw taped together. We managed to go a total of 28 ft and were tied for second.
