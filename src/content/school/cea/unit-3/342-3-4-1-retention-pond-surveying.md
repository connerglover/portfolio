The third part of the surveying work. We surveyed the retention pond behind the senior parking lot with the same professional equipment, then modelled it in OnShape to get its size, depth and volume, and documented the findings. We worked quickly and accurately and recorded everything, but did not take enough pictures — and had to watch out for the bees.
