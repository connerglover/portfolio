Joey Lodowsky, Johnny Collins and I toured the school's HVAC and compared it against how a house is served. We looked at the science wing's closet unit, the older large-diameter intake and outtake runs in Fine Arts, the chapel's small grates tucked out of sight feeding a major unit outside, the directly integrated grates and side-of-room unit in Mr. T's classroom, and the older equipment serving the locker rooms and athletic hallway.
