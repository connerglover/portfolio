A short presentation on four landscaping designs, two good and two bad. Moraine Hills in McHenry curates its planting to attract particular species, and the range of habitats supports a lot of wildlife along ten-plus miles of trails. The Arboretum of South Barrington is an open-air shopping centre named for the treescapes and gardens that replaced the tree farm that stood there. Eastway Park in Island Lake was one of the bad ones — the grass is cut extremely short and is mostly dead, and you have to drive across it to reach the boat launch.
