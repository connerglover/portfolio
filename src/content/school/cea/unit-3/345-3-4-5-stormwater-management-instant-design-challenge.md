Joey Lodowsky, Johnny Collins and I were given eight pieces of cut PVC pipe, six pieces of styrofoam and unlimited tape, and told to build the most efficient water transport system. We went with the old-fashioned aqueduct: a half-pipe run propped up with styrofoam, using the downward slope of the tables to carry the water. Our method of joining the pipes was probably the worst possible one and it went through most of Mr. T's tape, but it worked and we won.
