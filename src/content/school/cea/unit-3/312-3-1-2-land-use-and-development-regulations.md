Johnny and I worked through the land use and licensing requirements for the Keystone site: restroom counts, clear working space in front of electrical panels and HVAC units, exit counts and maximum travel distances, hallway and door widths, parking ratios including ADA van-accessible spaces, and where a dumpster can sit and how it has to be screened. We then listed the permits the project would need — an Indiana Construction Design Release, a Noblesville improvement location permit and building permit, a certificate of occupancy, and business registration — and estimated the filing and impact fees for the 9,594 square foot space.
