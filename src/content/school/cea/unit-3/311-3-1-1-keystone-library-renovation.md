The biggest project of the course. Johnny Collins and I redeveloped an industrial brownfield site in Noblesville, Indiana into a mixed-use building — two ground-floor retail stores and two second-floor condominiums. We brainstormed four candidate businesses per slot and scored them in a weighted decision matrix across construction cost, customer traffic, ADA compliance, room complexity and market demand; a fishing and outdoor gear shop and a kitchen supply store came out on top, helped along by the name combination. The Revit set covers floor plans, framing, foundations, section views, a green roof, curtain walls, the parking lot and stormwater management, with appendices for the code work and structural analysis.
