A walking tour of Carmel Catholic's own roofs and ceiling systems, photographing how each was built. Fine Arts turned out to be the most varied: the art room and performance spaces leave open-web steel joists, ducts and lighting grids exposed, while the music room is finished with acoustic tile and sound-absorbing panels and the hallway runs a plain lay-in tile grid. The engineering room uses the same exposed approach but painted dark, and the science wing has a skylight let into the deck.
