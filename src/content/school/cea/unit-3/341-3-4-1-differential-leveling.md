Angel Ricardo, Ayomide Akingbesote, Claire Susanto and I mapped as much of the garden as we could with an automatic level, rod and tripod, measuring landmarks against a benchmark we picked. We came out with the trees at 32 and 60 feet, the circle bench at 42, the Mary statue at 91, the rock bench at 112, the path circle at 123, the fountain at 136 and the yellow chairs at 140. We could not work out the distance to a drain from where we were set up.
