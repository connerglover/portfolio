I researched and sketched six foundation systems and described the conditions each suits: continuous strip footings with a foundation wall, spread footings with piers, slab-on-grade with a thickened edge, mat foundations, pile and pile cap, and cast-in-situ piles with grade beams. I then applied it to the Keystone site, which sits in flood Zone AE — the lowest floor has to be at or above base flood elevation, enclosed areas below it need flood vents, the materials below have to be flood-resistant, and the mechanical, electrical and plumbing equipment all has to be raised.
