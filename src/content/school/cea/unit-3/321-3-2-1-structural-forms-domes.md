Ayomide Akingbesote and I reported on the dome as a structural form. A dome works as a three-dimensional arch, spreading weight and pressure across a curved surface down to its supports. We traced it from the mammoth bone huts of around 19,000 BCE in what is now Ukraine, through the Roman Pantheon, to the Byzantines setting a dome on a square base at the Hagia Sophia, and on to modern examples like Spaceship Earth at Epcot.
