Johnny Collins and I had to build a beam out of straws and paper. We settled on an idea quickly — four trusses with the paper attached along the shorter top edge — and executed it. What we missed was how much straw that would take and how to stop the trusses flexing side to side. We traded a lot of tape away for a few extra straws and then ran out, and with no lateral support the beam buckled. I was cocky about the design.
