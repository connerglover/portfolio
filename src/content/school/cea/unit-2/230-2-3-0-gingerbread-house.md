We had to design a gingerbread house out of simple ingredients — graham crackers, frosting, pretzels and gumdrops — and then rebuild it as CAD in Revit. I started by optimising for the Revit side and made a simple square, but that first generation was neither polished nor structurally stable, so I redesigned it around my lake house. Hot glue held the second one together and I 3D scanned it. Going through multiple designs cost me time I did not need to spend.
