My first major project in Revit. Building the shed itself was not the hard part — the technical side was what got tedious. I made something a bit outside the box and learned how to place walls, doors and windows, customise the composition, build custom textures, and lay out sheets with different views. I finished the shed on time but sat on the slideshow for an obscene amount of time, and I held back on my original vision because I was still intimidated by the software.
