I built a roof onto a tissue box. The criteria called for a dormer, so I went with a gambrel with a dormer — the materials were easy to get, the design is simple, and it is the most common dormer roof. I set the roof angle at 45 degrees so I could use a 45-45-90 triangle and keep the trigonometry simple, which let me cut to real dimensions instead of guessing. I did not plan the sheathing, plywood, or shingles ahead of time and left all of that to the end. I 3D scanned the finished house.
