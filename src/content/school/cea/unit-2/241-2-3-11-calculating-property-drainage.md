John Krogman and I picked a city and presented on its flood plan. We chose Memphis, Tennessee, which sits on the Mississippi floodplain where three major waterways meet — in 2011 the river came close to its highest recorded level there. Memphis answers it with levees and concrete floodwalls, pumping stations that lift rainwater over the levees, a separate storm sewer system, restored wetlands along the Wolf River greenway, and building codes that restrict where and how you can build. Those defences largely held through the 2011 flood.
