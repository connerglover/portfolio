John Krogman and I built the tallest freestanding paper tower we could. I knew a fold that makes the paper more or less staple itself, so we made as many of those as possible and stacked them. The tower stood almost the whole time. Some of the folds were uneven and we were rushing to stabilise it at the end — stability matters just as much as height.
