I walked through Fairhaven, the neighborhood behind Carmel, photographing roofs and sorting them by type. I found at least one of every type we had covered: shed, gable, hip, gable with dormer, low-slope, gambrel, gable and valley, and hip and valley. A few of my identifications were stretched for the sake of completing the set, and I turned the write-up in late.
