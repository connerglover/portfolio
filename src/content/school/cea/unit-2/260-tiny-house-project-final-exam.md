The final exam for the semester. John Krogman and I designed a Habitat for Humanity home for a client, weighing affordability, accessibility, energy and water efficiency, and safety. Our clients, David and Sophia, wanted a simple energy-efficient ranch for themselves and their eight-year-old son Matthew — a garden for Sophia and space for Matthew to play soccer. We worked from hand sketches into Floorplanner and then into Revit, producing elevations, section views, renders, and the plumbing and electrical layouts.
