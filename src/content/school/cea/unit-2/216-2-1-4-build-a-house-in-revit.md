We had to design and model a house in Revit and then decorate it in the software of our choice. Mine was inspired by the townhomes at The Sanctuary at Lake Zurich — I was struck by what buyers were willing to pay for such a small house, which told me location and design matter more to them than square footage. So I set out to build a downsized version that could sell around $750,000 and be cheaper to produce. I originally stacked the bathroom and laundry directly above the kitchen so all the water ran through one area, but adding a garage forced me to redo the design. I built and furnished the whole thing in Revit.
