I picked Sauk County, Wisconsin, mostly because its plats of survey are easy to pull — the county has a dedicated app for them. I had Gemini pick a random address in the county, which gave me 602 Eisenhower Street in Baraboo, and then used Manus to track down the plat of survey for it. Even on the easiest county it took about seventeen minutes to find.
