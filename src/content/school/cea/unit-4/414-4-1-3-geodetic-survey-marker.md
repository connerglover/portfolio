The other half of 4.1.3 was to go find a real geodetic survey marker, and the trip did not go to plan. The only parking was a bar or a neighborhood with a reputation for slashing tires, the frozen lake I meant to cross did not look like it would hold my weight, and the only path along the road soaked my socks with murky water. NOAA's map and Google Maps could not agree on whether the marker was in the median before the roundabout or off the side of the road, and I found no sign of it either way — it may well have been buried under snow. I gave up on the field search and documented what I could find online instead.
