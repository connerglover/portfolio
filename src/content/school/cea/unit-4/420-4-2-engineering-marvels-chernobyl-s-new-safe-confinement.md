For the engineering marvels project I presented the New Safe Confinement over Reactor No. 4 at Chernobyl. The original Object Shelter went up within months of the April 1986 explosion and was never meant to be permanent; decades of structural decay left a real risk of radioactive material spreading across Europe. The replacement arch spans 257 metres — wide enough to cover the Statue of Liberty laid on her side — and runs 162 metres long. It was funded through the Chernobyl Shelter Fund with contributions from 45 nations, and was designed both to contain the reactor and to let the old shelter be dismantled safely underneath it.
