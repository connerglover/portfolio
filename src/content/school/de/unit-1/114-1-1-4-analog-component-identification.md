Reading components by sight. Decoded resistor colour bands into values and tolerances, then worked out the acceptable measured range for each of ten resistors given a ±5% or ±10% tolerance. Also identified capacitors from their markings, including the asymmetric −20%/+80% tolerance electrolytics come with.
