Practice converting between scientific notation, engineering notation and SI prefix notation — the difference being that engineering notation keeps the exponent a multiple of three, so it lines up with the prefixes you actually use on components. Worked through values from 3.56 nF-scale figures up to gigahertz, then converted between prefixes directly.
